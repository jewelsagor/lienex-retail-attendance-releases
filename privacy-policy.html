Lienex Retail Attendance — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Lienex Retail Attendance ("the app") is a staff attendance kiosk published by Lienex. A business installs it on a shop device so its employees can mark attendance with their face. This policy explains what the app handles and where that data goes.

Who controls the attendance data

The business using the app (your employer) chooses the attendance server the app sends records to and controls those records. Lienex does not receive attendance records, faces or locations through the app. Questions about records held by your employer should go to your employer.

What the app handles

DataWhyWhere it goes
Camera images of your face To recognise enrolled staff when they mark attendance Processed on the device only. Photos are deleted after each check and are never uploaded.
Face data (a numeric face template) To recognise an enrolled employee Stored on the device only. Never uploaded. Removed when the employee is deleted from the device or the app is uninstalled.
Staff ID, name and time of each attendance punch To record attendance Stored on the device and sent to your employer's attendance server.
Device location (precise or approximate) To confirm attendance was marked at the work site Read only while the attendance screen is open and only after you agree in the app. Sent with the punch to your employer's attendance server. Never collected in the background.
Administrator email and password To protect the app's admin screens Stored on the device only. The password is stored as a salted hash.
Diagnostics from Google ML Kit (device model, OS and app version, an installation identifier, performance and error data) Face detection is provided by Google ML Kit, which reports diagnostics and usage analytics Sent to Google over HTTPS. Google does not share it with third parties. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure.

What the app does not do

  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking by Lienex.
  • No sale or sharing of personal data with third parties.
  • No location collection in the background.

Security

Release versions of the app connect to the attendance server over HTTPS only, so data is encrypted in transit.

How to delete your data (Lienex Retail Attendance)

Employees using Lienex Retail Attendance can have their data deleted as follows:

  1. Face data on the shop device: ask the shop's administrator to open the app, go to Users, and tap the delete icon next to your name (Remove face from this device). Your face template is deleted at once.
  2. Attendance records on the shop device: the administrator can delete individual records in the Attendance tab. Uninstalling the app deletes everything the app stored on that device.
  3. Attendance records on your employer's server (staff ID, name, time and location of each punch): ask your employer. They control that server and delete records under their own retention policy.
  4. Need help? Email anzaragloballtd@gmail.com with the subject "Lienex Retail Attendance data deletion", your staff ID and your employer's name. We will help you reach the right administrator within 30 days.

What is deleted: face templates and attendance records as described above. What may be kept: attendance records your employer must keep by law, for as long as your employer's policy requires. Lienex itself holds no copy of your data.

Children

The app is a workplace tool for employees and is not directed at children.

We will update this page if the app's data handling changes. The date above shows the latest version.
